# Equity Research & Valuation Projects

I'm an Economics major at the University of Regina. These are a few projects I've worked on through URI Portfolio Management and my economics coursework. Some were team projects and others were completed on my own.

## Equity Research

### [Tourmaline Oil (TOU) — CFA Institute Research Challenge](Tourmaline_Oil/)
Team research on a Canadian natural gas producer. We used NAV and comparable-company valuations to support a buy recommendation with a C$65.47 price target (12.2% potential upside). Our team placed **top 3 among eight Western Canadian schools**.

[Research report](Tourmaline_Oil/Tourmaline_Equity_Research_Report.pdf) · [Pitch deck](Tourmaline_Oil/Tourmaline_Investment_Pitch_Deck.pdf)

### [Capital Power (CPX) — Equity Research](Capital_Power/)
Research completed with another analyst for URI Portfolio Management. We valued Capital Power using DCF, SOTP and dividend discount models, and presented a buy recommendation with a C$75 price target (approximately 22.5% potential upside).

[Research report](Capital_Power/Capital_Power_Equity_Research_Report.pdf) · [Pitch deck](Capital_Power/Capital_Power_Investment_Pitch_Deck.pdf) · [Excel valuation model](Capital_Power/Capital_Power_Valuation_Model.xlsx)
