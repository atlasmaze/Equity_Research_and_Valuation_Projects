# Capital Power (CPX) — Equity Research

**URI Portfolio Management | March 2026**

I worked with another analyst to research Capital Power and present a buy recommendation to the Investment Council. Our **C$75.00 price target** represented approximately **22.5% potential upside** from the C$61.20 share price used in the report.

## Research and valuation
- Assessed Capital Power's generation assets, financial performance, and exposure to power markets in Canada and the U.S.
- Used DCF, sum-of-the-parts (SOTP), and dividend discount models to estimate a share value
- Examined contracting, electricity demand, financial risks, and valuation sensitivities
