# SaskPower Rose Valley Wind Project — Cost-Benefit Analysis

**ECON 351 | University of Regina | December 2025**

I completed this project for my Cost-Benefit Analysis course. The report looks at the proposed **200 MW Rose Valley wind project** and compares its costs and benefits with continued natural gas generation over 30 years.

## What I analyzed
- Construction, operating, and decommissioning costs
- Avoided emissions and conventional generation costs
- Bull, base, and bear cases, using 3%, 5%, and 7% social discount rates

## Base-case results

| Discount rate | Net present value |
| --- | ---: |
| 3% | C$309.0M |
| 5% | C$147.1M |
| 7% | C$33.1M |

The base case showed a positive NPV at each discount rate. Under the bear-case assumptions, NPV was negative at 7%.
