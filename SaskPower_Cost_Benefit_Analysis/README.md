# SaskPower Rose Valley Wind Project — Cost-Benefit Analysis

An individual academic project completed for ECON 351 at the University of Regina in December 2025. I evaluated the proposed 200 MW Rose Valley wind facility against a baseline of continued natural-gas generation over a 30-year horizon.

## Methodology
- Modelled construction, operating, and decommissioning costs alongside avoided fossil-generation costs and emissions damages
- Evaluated social net present value (NPV) at discount rates of 3%, 5%, and 7%
- Tested bull, base, and bear scenarios using alternative assumptions for wind output and other project impacts
- Considered how costs and benefits would be distributed among Saskatchewan residents and communities

## Results
The **base-case social NPV** was estimated at **C$309.0M (3%)**, **C$147.1M (5%)**, and **C$33.1M (7%)**. The project remained positive in the base case at all three rates, although the bear case became negative at 7%.
