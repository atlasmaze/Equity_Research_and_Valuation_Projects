# SaskPower Rose Valley Wind Project — Cost-Benefit Analysis

Academic project for ECON 351 at the University of Regina.

## Approach
- 30-year cost-benefit analysis of a proposed 200 MW wind project
- Assessment of construction, operating, land, tax, and decommissioning costs
- Evaluation of avoided emissions benefits and NPV sensitivity at 3%, 5%, and 7% discount rates

## Result
Estimated $1.67B in net economic benefits under the project's assumptions.
