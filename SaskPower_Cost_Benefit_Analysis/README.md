# SaskPower Rose Valley Wind Project | Cost-Benefit Analysis

**ECON 351 | University of Regina | December 2025**

For this individual academic project, I evaluated the proposed 200 MW Rose Valley wind facility against a baseline of continued natural gas generation over 30 years.

## Methodology
- Modelled construction, operating, and decommissioning costs and avoided generation and emissions damages
- Calculated social net present value (NPV) at 3%, 5%, and 7% discount rates
- Tested bull, base, and bear scenarios and considered the distribution of costs and benefits across Saskatchewan

## Results
| Social discount rate | Base-case NPV |
| --- | ---: |
| 3% | C$309.0M |
| 5% | C$147.1M |
| 7% | C$33.1M |

The base case produced a positive NPV at all three discount rates. In the bear case, NPV was negative at 7%.
